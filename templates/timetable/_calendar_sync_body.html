Google Calendar

  1. On a computer, open calendar.google.com.
  2. Next to Other calendars, press + then From URL.
  3. Paste the link above and press Add calendar. It shows on your phone too.

iPhone, iPad or Mac

  1. Press Open in my calendar app above, then Subscribe.
  2. Or go to Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar, and paste the link.

Outlook

  1. In Outlook on the web, open the calendar and choose Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
  2. Paste the link and give it a name.
